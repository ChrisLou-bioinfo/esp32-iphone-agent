ESP32 Agent needs a small ESP32-S3 USB board (open-source firmware) plugged into the iPhone. The board receives Screen Mirroring (the model's view of the screen) and acts as a standard USB HID mouse/keyboard; the app talks to it over Bluetooth LE. The app uses no private APIs: it never reads other apps' content itself, it only sees what the user mirrors to the board.

Demo video of the full flow (connect, mirror, run a task, replay): <VIDEO_URL>

Without a board: turn on Settings › Demo data, then quit and reopen the app. It fakes a board, a finished run and the whole setup, so every screen can be reviewed.
